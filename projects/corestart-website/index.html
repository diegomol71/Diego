What We Offer

Two ways we help your organization thrive

CoreStart offers made-to-measure Corporate Wellness programs and strategic Consulting services designed around your people, your budget, and your goals.

Corporate Wellness Programs

Five made-to-measure programs that build a lasting Culture of Health, with communication tools, smart incentives, and focus groups to drive real employee participation.

  • Creating a Healthy Workplace — redesigning food and drink options, and incentives for movement and relaxation.
  • Health Risk Assessment (HRA) — questionnaires and biometric screening with personalized feedback.
  • Lifestyle Management — workshops and group challenges on sleep, stress, smoking, and chronic disease.
  • Movement & Physical Fitness — on-site or external activity programs with long-term, measurable results.
  • Nutrition Workshops — from clean eating basics to mindful eating, delivered in an engaging, social format.

Corporate Health Coaching

A hands-on, clinical intervention based on motivational interviewing, education, and goal setting — one-on-one or in small groups, usually over a 6-to-8-week program.

Having a dedicated coach significantly increases the odds of lasting behavior change, giving your organization a stronger return on its wellness investment and a real, lasting impact on employee wellbeing.

Consulting Services

Most organizations know they need to change but aren't sure how to make the first move. We bring a global network of experts to help you take that step.

  • Introductory Consultation — expert guidance to uncover growth opportunities and actionable next steps.
  • Strategic Planning Sessions — Assessment, Planning, and Execution built around your business.
  • Sustainability Analysis — a multidisciplinary review of the environmental, economic, and social dimensions of your organization.
